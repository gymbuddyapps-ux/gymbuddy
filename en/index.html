How it works

Everything you need to train

  1. Classes from verified gyms

    Partner gyms post their classes with the time, price and seats. Tap Interested or contact the gym. The gym sees a count, never your name.

  2. Obour gyms and their perks

    See the partner gyms, and get a free session or money off a membership with a code from the app.

  3. Not only the gym

    Post a run, a football game, padel or a swim. Set the place and the spots, and people near you join.

  4. All your requests in one place

    Train requests, chats and the sessions you agreed on.
